Data types collected

Two per-type questions confuse people — answer them for this kit as:

  • Processed ephemerally? → No for everything here. Finlytics stores its data: auth in Supabase, the ledger in Postgres and Room, events in PostHog and Firebase.
  • Required, or users choose? → Users can choose only where a real in-app opt-out exists (Settings → Privacy → Share usage analytics). Everything else is required for the app to work.

Nothing is marked Shared. Play defines sharing as transfer to a third party that uses the data for its own purposes, and explicitly excludes a service provider processing on our behalf. Supabase, Firebase, PostHog and RevenueCat are all service providers here. There is no ad network, attribution SDK or data broker in this app.
